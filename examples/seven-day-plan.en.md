# Demo: a side-project idea becomes a seven-day test

**Fictional example.** This offline demo includes no completed market research, outreach, or payments. Numbers, prices, and thresholds are proposed test settings. See [the Chinese demo](seven-day-plan.zh-CN.md).

## Input

> I know Java and AI application development, have two hours per day and CNY 300 this week, and know a few shop owners. Map possible side-project directions and give me a seven-day validation plan.

## Current judgment

First test whether shop owners would try a paid manual service that reduces time spent preparing weekly business summaries. It fits the stated access and can be delivered manually. **There is no evidence yet of this pain or willingness to pay, so prioritize a test before building a full system.**

| ID | Context | Type and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Java and AI development skills | Fictional self-report; work samples/delivery unconfirmed |
| E2 | Two hours daily, CNY 300 weekly cap | Fictional self-report; maximum 14 hours in seven days |
| E3 | Knows several shop owners | Fictional self-report; interview availability and business unknown |

Unknowns include actual workflow, pain, losses, buyer, available data, and willingness to share anonymized samples.

## Opportunity map

| Direction | Customer/problem assumption | Alternatives | Access/delivery | Cost/gaps | Tradeoff |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Manual business summary | Shop owners spend too much time organizing weekly information | Self, staff, existing software (specific tools unverified) | Existing contacts, manual sample | Within weekly caps; pain/payment unknown | Test first based on E3 access |
| General AI knowledge-base product | A business segment struggles to find documents | Existing search/knowledge tools (products/prices unverified) | No supplied channel; permissions/deployment unknown | Scope unknown | Defer until a customer is identified |
| Content organization service | Some creators struggle with raw material | Manual work, tools (unverified) | No creator access supplied | Pain/acquisition unknown | Explore access first |
| No new project this week | Preserve time and cash | Status quo | No delivery | CNY 0, zero experiment hours | Defer if recruitment is infeasible |

Without browsing, retain these unknowns and list research tasks: actual store workflows, official relevant tools/prices, and delivery constraints. Do not invent competitors or market size.

## Critical assumption H1

Of four participants who complete a conversation and genuinely need weekly summaries, at least one, after viewing an anonymized sample, confirms the scope, **CNY 100 per delivery** test price, and date of a paid trial.

CNY 100 is a proposed offer, not a market average. A commitment needs a verifiable explicit answer; praise and “maybe later” do not count. The primary metric is credible purchase commitments among qualified participants. Record actual payments separately; commitments are not completed sales.

Fewer than four qualified observations means insufficient evidence. Distinguish recruiting failure from absent demand and do not add unrelated friends to the denominator.

## Seven-day plan

| Day | Action | Output | Hours | Cash cap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Capture context, sampling criteria, invitation and interview draft | Profile, H1, question guide | 1.5 | CNY 0 |
| 2 | User conducts authorized invitations and schedules interviews | Recruitment log and qualified count | 2 | CNY 0 |
| 3 | Ask about the latest task, workflow, time, and consequences | Anonymous observations; preserve any design changes | 2 | CNY 0 |
| 4 | If pain is established and sample use authorized, prepare a manual sample | Sample and delivery time | 2 | CNY 80 |
| 5 | Show sample, explain scope and test price, record explicit answers | Commitments/rejection reasons | 2 | CNY 0 |
| 6 | Complete missing observations or an authorized trial delivery | Usage/payment/delivery observations | 1.5 | CNY 70 |
| 7 | Compare H1, sample, spending, and thresholds | Continue/adjust/stop/gather-evidence decision | 1 | CNY 0 |

Total: **12 hours and CNY 150**, leaving **2 hours and CNY 150** of contingency, within the **14-hour/CNY 300** caps. End unnecessary production early if recruitment or pain is absent; spending the full budget is not an outcome.

Ask about a real recent event, workaround, time, and consequences before showing a solution. Avoid leading questions about whether an AI report sounds appealing. Outreach, payments, and publication are execution actions governed by authorization; this example only plans them.

## Preset rules

- **Continue:** at least four qualified observations, at least one explicit purchase commitment, and sample delivery within caps. Next test actual payment and delivery cost.
- **Adjust:** genuine pain but no purchase commitment at this scope/price. Test scope or payment next, preserving the current outcome.
- **Stop this proposal:** four observations show no clear pain, or delivery repeatedly exceeds caps. Do not infer that the entire industry lacks demand.
- **Insufficient evidence:** inadequate sample, missing records, or unexecuted plan. Identify the missing stage.
- **Hard stop:** 14 hours or CNY 300 reached; further investment is the user's decision.

## Review handoff

Observed results: **pending**. Record recruitment, qualified denominator, pain observations, commitments, payments, sample time, and spend. If saving, create the profile and `projects/shop-summary/` opportunity, experiment, and review records; index only existing files.

Today's action: identify owners willing to discuss their latest summary task and prepare five questions about actual experience.

## Example follow-up

> Fictional practice data: six contacted, three qualified interviews completed, two mentioned difficulty, one praised the sample, no explicit purchase commitments. Eight hours and CNY 50 spent. Review against the original thresholds.

Expected judgment: **insufficient evidence; willingness to pay remains unestablished**. Three observations fall short of four, with zero purchase commitments. Praise is not a sale. Additional observations can fit within the remaining six hours/CNY 250, or the user may end the round. Do not lower the original threshold after the outcome.
