# Life baseline demo (fictional, not an observed model run)

A maintainer-authored illustration, not a real consultation, medical assessment, market study, or passed behavioral evaluation.

## Input

I have five years of development experience, want an AI side project without quitting, and want time for learning and family. After work, care, and necessary rest, I have seven hours weekly and CNY 200 for new actions. I already walk and want to maintain that habit. Discuss only this budget, not my income/assets. Give me a two-week plan in chat without saving files.

## Judgment

Try a small learning artifact and an opportunity investigation. Experience does not establish demand, and the available context cannot establish overall health or finances. Use existing tools, do not quit, and keep each week within seven hours.

## Seven themes

| Theme | Known context | Gap/limit |
|---|---|---|
| Cognition | Interest in exploration | AI business viability is a hypothesis; find possible counterevidence |
| Decisions | User does not want to quit | Compare status quo, learning only, learning plus investigation |
| AI/knowledge | Self-reported development experience | Actual AI capability/tools unknown; start a small artifact |
| Business | Interest, not validated demand | Customer, payment reason, access, and delivery costs unknown |
| Wealth | CNY 200 new-action cap | Other financial details not discussed; no independence forecast |
| Health | Existing walking habit | No clinical assessment or added treatment/training |
| Experience | Learning and family preferences | Shared activities require mutual availability/consent |

## Shared weekly plan

Week two adapts to observations under the same cap. Figures are proposed allocations, not observed spending.

| Action | Weekly time | New cash | Observation/adjustment |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Build and explain a small tool, including learning/preparation | 3 h | CNY 0 | Reduce scope if existing tools cannot do it; no automatic subscription |
| Investigate one audience's problem and alternatives | 2 h | CNY 0 | Mark unsourced claims pending; investigate access before inventing customer feedback |
| Agree on one shared family activity | 0.5 h | CNY 0 | Includes coordination; reschedule if not mutually feasible |
| Briefly log existing walking and routine | 0.5 h | CNY 0 | No inference of medical effects |
| Combined review, notes, next-week planning | 1 h | CNY 0 | Compare actual workload; distinguish scope from estimation issues |
| **Total** | **7 h** | **CNY 0** | **CNY 200 unused, no time buffer** |

The first three are priorities; logging and review are shared overhead. Existing obligations/rest/walking are already outside the available seven hours per user input. If additional obligations appear, reduce the tool task before displacing recovery. Proposed two-week totals are 14 hours and CNY 0; the CNY 200 new-action cap remains unused.

## Review after two weeks

Check the learning artifact and explanation, sourced problem/alternatives/access path, mutual agreement and workload, and which assumptions changed. No demand or payment has yet been validated. Continue, reduce scope, or defer according to actual observations.

These are proposed review rules, not confirmed user agreements. No files, model calls, customer contacts, or reminders were created.
