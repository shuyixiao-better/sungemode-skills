# Sources and product design

Inspired by [Alan Shao's public interview with Justin Sun about opportunities in the AI era](https://x.com/AlanShao111/status/2108406850727076182). Implementation used the full transcript supplied by the project owner in the referenced naming conversation and checked the segments below. The transcript may contain transcription errors; timestamps refer to the supplied version. The public video was not independently verified line by line.

| Inspiration | Transcript time | Our implementation |
|---|---|---|
| Preserve context in Markdown so another AI can continue | 00:07:28–00:07:48 | Personal profile, workspace index, cross-agent handoff |
| Analyze current circumstances and adjust with new data | 00:17:21–00:21:18 | Constraint-aware opportunity choices and review |
| Explore the map with scouts before committing resources | 00:43:31–00:45:16 | Customer, alternatives, channels, and cost mapping |
| Try an experience before deciding on further investment | 03:19:11–03:19:29 | Small experiments and continue/stop conditions |

The seven-day default, E/H IDs, spending caps, evidence classes, preset thresholds, five skills, and templates are Owen's product design, not interview quotations or preexisting software features. Seven days is configurable; the interview's discussion of longer-term data collection does not require users to wait months before starting.

The interview inspires a method; it does not establish demand, competitor pricing, policy, or business viability. Verify those claims separately when using the skill or mark them unknown. Demos are fictional and do not represent customer feedback.
