# Opportunity scouting

Default to English; honor explicit language preferences. Gather information before committing resources. This skill stands alone.

## Method

1. Establish goals, available time, spending cap, capabilities, and reachable customers from supplied context/profile. Ask up to three pivotal questions together; mark remaining gaps unknown.
2. Compare a few feasible directions plus doing nothing/keeping the status quo. Define the customer, pain scenario, current workaround, reason to pay, first acquisition channel, and delivery method.
3. Verify decision-relevant competitor, pricing, and market claims with available browsing, preferring official pages and original customer feedback. Record E IDs, URLs, access date, currency/period/region, and scope. Without browsing, provide a provisional map and verification route, never pretend completed research.
4. Separate facts, inferences tied to E IDs, testable H assumptions, and unknowns. Competitor prices do not establish your revenue; technology popularity does not establish demand; reposts of one source are not independent evidence.
5. Compare demand evidence, customer access, delivery cost, personal fit, and budget. Explain rankings and counterevidence. Transparent scores can assist ranking but are not success probabilities. Weak evidence supports a test priority, not a validated recommendation.
6. Propose one critical assumption, the cheapest useful test, time/spending caps, and continue/stop conditions. Thresholds are proposed experiment rules, not industry facts. Do not start outreach or development automatically.

## Output and records

Use [the opportunity template](assets/opportunity.en.md) for constraints, comparisons, evidence, recommendation, assumption, and test. Never label unknown demand validated; without direct feedback, customer validation remains pending.

When asked to save, write `.sungemode/projects/<project-slug>/opportunity.md` or the chosen path, preserve earlier judgments and change reasons, and update an existing index. Otherwise deliver in chat. Reuse E/H IDs and append new evidence IDs. End with one action the user can start tomorrow.
