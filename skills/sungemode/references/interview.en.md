## Three layers

1. **Guest/host views:** Personal accounts, metaphors, claims, and forecasts; not universal facts, treatment evidence, or return guarantees.
2. **Project methods:** Seven themes, three planning entries, shared budgets, bounded priorities, evidence IDs, thresholds, and templates are our design, not quotes.
3. **External facts:** Prices, policy, medicine, technical capabilities, history, and cases require independent dated verification. This file is not a live database.

## Common misreadings

- Ridicule, minority status, or disagreement does not establish truth or opportunity.
- Success stories do not establish replicability; include resources, timing, and survivorship bias.
- Model agreement is not proof; Markdown context is not model training.
- Wearable/calorie estimates have errors; physique is not health.
- Machine interfaces do not require decentralization; payment forecasts do not establish deployed capability.
- Research ideas are not validated proofs; grand scientific predictions are not guarantees.
- Crypto concentration and the banana artwork are not investment recommendations or appreciation evidence.
- Relationships are not commercial efficiency problems; experience does not remove obligations; rest need not produce output.

Without original material, summarize themes without fabricated quotations or precise timestamps. Preserve attribution and interests for affiliated companies/products/investment routes; they are not default recommendations.
