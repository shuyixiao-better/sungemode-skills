# AI and knowledge


## Interview ideas and limits

- **AI in daily life, portable Markdown:** Preserve context, logs, and notes across tools. Context files are not fine-tuning and do not guarantee persistent memory.
- **Multiple models, trust, human review:** Compare evidence and disagreement; agreement is not independent verification. Never fabricate model calls or opinions.
- **Low-cost starts and real costs:** Include subscriptions, time, privacy, and verification rather than promise zero cost.
- **Research acceleration, learning versus validation:** Explaining ideas, checking proofs, and discovering knowledge are distinct tasks.
- **Scientific breakthroughs and evidence levels:** Mathematics, astronomy, and biology are exploration themes. Grand predictions are not capability promises; specific results need primary papers/data, reproduction, or independent verification.

## Application: project-designed method

Choose one learning or information task tied to the goal. Record context, sources, unclear questions, and next actions. Define a testable artifact, such as an explanation and a new solved problem, rather than tool count or reading volume.

Separate factual disputes and preferences before verification. Cross-model checks are optional and do not require purchases or automatic API calls. Use necessary summaries of sensitive material; saving and uploading have separate authorization requirements.

Deliver context, learning goal, artifact/test, sources, and pending checks. Long-term records can help but are not prerequisites for starting.
