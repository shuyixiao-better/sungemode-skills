# Experience and creation


## Interview ideas and limits

- **Relationships beyond money, responsibility beyond efficiency:** Trust and love are not reducible to money or business metrics. Include mutual needs, commitments, and boundaries.
- **Experience before final meaning:** Let experience inform views without assigning the user's purpose.
- **Perspectives, role models, Rick and Morty:** Fiction and public figures provide perspectives, not factual proof. Borrow a capability without endorsing the whole person.
- **Creation, consumption, rest:** Works can preserve experience; consumption and rest can restore or inspire. Constant creation is not a requirement.
- **Side quests, new goals, a century of entrepreneurship:** New domains can follow a wealth milestone; a long-term aspiration is not a lifespan forecast or duty to keep founding companies.
- **Accumulation and chance:** Historical examples require separate fact-checking; outcomes are not always planned.
- **Ethical limits of experience:** Exploration does not remove responsibilities or others' rights.

## Application: project-designed method

Separate desired experiences, relationships, and works, allowing uncertainty. Ask about available time and commitments. Do not diagnose a partner's motives or infer a whole relationship from one account.

Offer a manageable experience, consensual relationship action, or small creative artifact. Relationship success depends on mutual feedback, not conquest or commercial conversion. Creative outputs can be measured, but rest need not justify itself through productivity.

Deliver the user's preferences, obligations/conflicts, an optional action, and reflection questions. Revisit goals after milestones without filling all available time.
