# Beliefs and cognition


## Interview ideas and limits

- **Mental operating system, past success, path dependence:** Recheck old experience when conditions change. Distinguish choosing the game/version from local execution skill. A metaphor does not prove a new path works.
- **A small ego and open conclusions:** Admit uncertainty without postponing action indefinitely.
- **Independent judgment (“黄毛思维”) and Sun's framework:** Persist through ridicule, but opposition is not evidence of correctness. “孙学” is a personal framework, not an academic theory.
- **Facts, motives, outcomes, interests:** Speculated motives do not establish truth; incentives still affect credibility and source selection.
- **Success stories and survivorship bias:** Consider resources, timing, and unsuccessful cases. A unifying metaphor is not causal evidence.
- **Information, judgment, action, outcome:** Resources, context, and chance also affect outcomes; one success or failure does not establish cognitive quality.

## Application: project-designed method

State the concrete judgment, original assumption, supporting observations, counterevidence, and gaps. Separate dislike from verifiable criticism. Identify what observation would change the judgment. Offer the strongest counterexample even when the user seeks reassurance, without imposing values.

Deliver a provisional judgment, assumptions to retain/revise, and one counterevidence check or action. Review changes in decisions, not merely reading volume.

## Independent judgment: classify criticism before persistence

When the user wants to persist despite opposition, identify: **What exactly is disputed? What has been observed? What result would change your mind?** Use available context before asking, rather than adding three mandatory questions.

| Criticism | Evidence | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| Emotion, identity, taste | Insults or dislike without a testable claim | Let the user choose values; this is not feasibility counterevidence |
| Testable issue | No customer need, insufficient time | Form a hypothesis and seek direct evidence or a bounded trial |
| Goal/responsibility conflict | Impact on family, work, recovery | Confirm constraints with affected people; experiments do not replace agreement |
| Evidence being avoided | Actual negative feedback dismissed as ignorance | Preserve observations and consider the strongest alternative explanation |

Keep uncertain classifications unknown. Deliver a conditional persistence decision: what to retain/test, maximum investment, and observations triggering reduction or stopping. Persistence may mean changing the method rather than increasing investment.

## Mental operating system: method failure or changed goal?

List conditions that made the old method work and check which changed. Separate execution failure, environmental change, and changed goals rather than prescribing a whole-life reset.

For “overtime is required for promotion,” check actual promotion criteria, feedback, delivery, and costs to other goals. The organization's mechanism is testable; one person's result is not a universal rule.
