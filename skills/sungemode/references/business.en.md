# Business and technology


## Interview ideas and limits

- **Real value and global supply/demand:** Start with customer needs and delivery; include acquisition and fulfillment costs across regions.
- **Token relay anecdote:** A reported pricing gap is not feasibility evidence. Check terms, data handling, reliability, costs, and applicable rules.
- **Machine-readable money and agent payments:** Centralized services also expose machine interfaces; decentralization is not required for machine readability. Verify current documentation, authorization, and deployment rather than treating predictions as available capabilities.
- **AI and life sciences:** Separate technical potential from customers, data access, expertise, and validation cycles.
- **Digital expression, the banana artwork, symbolic value and IP:** Attention, ownership, transactions, and returns are distinct. The anecdote does not establish appreciation for digital assets generally.
- **Distributed publishing and information quality:** Lower publishing barriers do not establish reliable information.

## Application: project-designed method

Use [the project workflow](project-flow.en.md) for customers, alternatives, acquisition, and delivery. Treat technical trends as hypotheses; documentation establishes capability, not demand. For personal IP, separately test audience, content value, distribution, and subsequent actions. Traffic forecasts are judgments, not fabricated platform statistics.

Deliver options, demand evidence, a critical assumption, a bounded experiment, and stop conditions. Drafting is distinct from publishing; payments and customer contact require user authorization.
